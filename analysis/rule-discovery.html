What the representation must preserve

  • Keep raw wording and normative force alongside interpretation.
  • Store applicability separately from requirement: retailer, channel/network, destination, department, PO type, freight terms, carrier/mode, product attributes and dates.
  • Preserve physical nesting and relationships: shipment, delivery, PO, pallet, master carton, carton, inner pack and item.
  • Keep identifier strings, application identifiers and barcode symbology distinct; retain leading zeros.
  • Represent conditions with AND/OR, units, comparison operators, deadlines and explicit exceptions; unresolved boundary cases stay unresolved.
  • Link an exception to the specific rule it changes; do not assume later upload wins.
  • Record data needed to evaluate each rule, human/visual evidence requirements, and missing-information states.
  • Keep fees and consequences separate from requirements and from guarantees of enforcement.
