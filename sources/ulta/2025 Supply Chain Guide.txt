SECTION 4: EDI (ELECTRONIC DATA INTERCHANGE) 
Ulta Beauty requires all Brand Partners to be EDI compliant within 30 days after executing 
the Vendor Purchasing Agreement (VPA) or Commercial Terms document, unless 
otherwise noted in the Vendor Purchasing Agreement (VPA) or Commercial Terms 
document by an authorized Ulta Beauty associate. Ulta Beauty utilizes OpenText as the 
technology platform to enable EDI processes. 
4.1 EDI Requirements 
 All Brand Partners must complete the Trading Partner Profile survey and accept the 
Pricing Agreement with OpenText.
 There is a one-time EDI testing fee associated with OpenText. If you need to use the 
OpenText web portal to share EDI documents, different fees apply. Refer to Section 4.5 
OpenText Fee Structure for details.
 New Brand Partners must respond to the Onboarding email invitation sent by the 
OpenText Compliance Service team to begin the EDI enablement process.
 All Brand Partners must register with FIS Integrated Payables (Ulta Beauty’s 3rd party 
provider for ACH pay method). Contact agp.jax.vendorenrollment@fisglobal.com or 
call 877-330-4950 to begin registration. It’s fast, easy and no cost to you. After 
registration is complete with FIS Integrated Payables, Brand Partners must contact 
FIS@ulta.com to finalize the ACH set up with Ulta Beauty.
New Brand Partners pending enablement onto the OpenText network must submit Invoices 
through the USPS mail to the following address:
Ulta Beauty, Inc. 
Attention: Accounts Payable 
1000 Remington Blvd., Suite 120 
Bolingbrook, IL 60440 
4.2 Required EDI Transactions
The following EDI transactions will enable both parties to conduct business with a minimum 
of manual intervention:
 850 Purchase Order
 855 Purchase Order Acknowledgement 
 860 Purchase Order Change Request
 856 ASN - Advanced Shipping Notification
o One ASN per PO, per delivery is required regardless of shipment method 
(TL/LTL/Parcel)
o For Blanket or Mega POs that do not fit on one truck, each truck/shipment 
against the PO, is required to have its own unique ASN submitted
o When shipping more than one PO on a pallet, each PO will need to have its own 
ASN but reference the shipment ID. Refer to section 15.2 Pallet Loading and 
Wrapping.
o Multiple Ulta Beauty vendor numbers to one ASN are not accepted
o ASN must be sent prior to shipment arrival at Ulta Beauty DC
 810 Invoice
 GSI-128 Labels with proper SSCC-18 codes are required for pallet and carton labels. 
Refer to Section 14: Packing & Label Requirements for GS1-128 label standards.

4.3 EDI Transaction Methods
Brand Partners may select from two EDI methods for data exchange through OpenText: 
OpenText EDI Integrated:

capabilities and support.

access POs, submit ASN’s and invoices, and print Ulta compliant GS1-128 shipping

have established EDI capabilities and resources.

SECTION 7: EDI/OPENTEXT FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS 
1. If I am a new Brand Partner and have not completed the EDI set-up process with OpenText yet, 
how will I receive Purchase Orders? 
• PO’s will be emailed from your designated Ulta Beauty Inventory Analyst until you are 
transacting through OpenText.
• Brand Partners will submit Invoices to the address listed in Section 4.1 until you are able to 
send them electronically.
2. Can I use an EDI 3rd Party Provider?
• Yes. Note: there may be fees associated with a 3rd party provider. 
3. What are the dates being sent on the Purchase Order?
Ulta Beauty exchanges the following dates on Purchase Orders: 
• Requested Ship Date: The date the shipment must be ready for Carrier pick up. 
This date applies for Collect Freight terms. Requested Ship Date = PO Submission 
date + pick/pack/ship calendar days. 
• Requested Delivery Date (RDD): The date the shipment must be delivered to the 
designated Ulta Beauty Distribution Center. This date applies for Prepaid Freight 
terms. RDD = Ship Date + Pre-set transit time in calendar days unless that day falls 
on a weekend or holiday.

SECTION 9: SAFETY DATA SHEETS 
9.1 SDS Requirement
Ulta Beauty requires all formulated products to be registered with UL WERCSmart
ULTA Beauty has selected UL WERCSmart to manage the secure collection and evaluation 
of critical data for the safe shipping, storage, and disposal of hazardous or potentially 
hazardous goods. Your organization's partnership is crucial, and we appreciate your 
support in helping ULTA Beauty maintain a compliant and safe environment for both 
employees and consumers.
9.2 UL WERCSmart
Brand Partner’s Responsibility
ULTA Beauty requires all formulated products to be registered in the WERCSmart platform 
along with the corresponding Safety Data Sheet. While the registration process is 
customized for each product type, information typically required includes the formulation, 
product-level attributes, UPC information, and compliance documentation (SDS, Label, 38.3 
Test Summary).
The WERCSmart Process
Once submitted, your data and compliance documentation will be evaluated 
securely by the WERCSmart Assessment Team. Any discrepancies found through 
human review or WERCSmart’s automated rule steam will be addressed with your 
support.
Next Steps
If your organization does not have a WERCSmart account, you will be prompted to 
create one. Existing WERCSmart suppliers should make sure that all formulated 
products sold at ULTA Beauty are properly registered by adding UPCs to new or 
existing product registrations. https://ulwercsmart.com/
Support Options
UL Onboarding Specialists will reach out to new suppliers upon account creation to 
introduce you to WERCSmart and guide you through next steps. For ongoing 
WERCSmart support, UL offers two options: 
• General Customer Support: FreshDesk tickets, phone calls and chat
• Agency Support Services: a premium service offering registration support and 
an assigned agent.

SECTION 10: PURCHASE ORDER REQUIREMENTS
10.1 Purchase Order Requirement
Ulta Beauty requires all line items on the Purchase Order fulfill 100% and deliver to the 
designated Distribution Center on the ‘Requested Delivery date’ (RDD) for Prepaid freight 
or be ready for Carrier pick-up on the ‘Requested Ship Date’ for Collect freight. 
Overages Policy
Ulta Beauty reserves the right to execute on one or more of the following actions, per 
receiving an unauthorized overage and/or item(s) not listed on the Purchase Order:
 Accept merchandise shipped in excess of the purchase order quantity. Ulta Beauty 
reserves the right to keep the goods at no charge. 
 Request disposition (RA#, destroy in field, etc.). Disposition is required within 2 
business days of notification. Refer to Section 23: Shipment Non-Compliance Fees
for associated fees. If disposition is not received within 2 business days or if 
merchandise cannot be returned for any reason merchandise will be disposed of at 
the discretion of Ulta Beauty and any disposal cost charged back to the Brand 
Partner.
 Assess Overage Chargeback fee per Purchase Order, per Brand Partner tier, at a 
variable rate. Refer to Section 22: Chargeback Program.
10.2 Purchase Order Dates and Definitions
Ulta Beauty exchanges 2 dates on all our Purchase Orders: 
• Requested Ship Date: Date the shipment must be ready for Carrier pick up. This 
date is applicable on Purchase Orders with Collect freight terms. 
Requested Ship Date = PO Submission date + pick/pack calendar days. 
• Requested Delivery Date (RDD): Date the shipment must be delivered to the 
designated Ulta Beauty Distribution Center. This date is applicable on Purchase 
Orders with Prepaid freight terms. 
RDD = Ship Date + Pre-set transit time in calendar days unless that day falls on a 
weekend or holiday. 
At the discretion of the Ulta Beauty Buying and Inventory teams, the Cancel Date may also 
be exchanged:
• Cancel Date: The last day the PO can be shipped before it is considered canceled. 
Cancel Date = RDD + # of days chosen by Ulta Beauty Inventory and Buying 
teams.

The Purchase Order lead-time is agreed upon and documented on the Vendor Purchasing 
Agreement (VPA) or Commercial Terms document.

10.4 Purchase Order Management Process
If a Brand Partner cannot fulfill the original Purchase Order line item quantity 100%, you are 
required to notify Ulta Beauty within 2 business days of PO transmission utilizing the EDI 
855 PO Acknowledgement transaction. 
This includes all items on allocation and any long term out of stocks. 
Requirements:
 EDI 855 PO Acknowledgment on all live PO 850s
If NO PO Change request is submitted or submitted >2 business days, Fill Rate Compliance
is calculated from the Original PO line quantity.
New Brand Partners who have not completed the EDI set-up process or existing Brand 
Partners with prior Ulta Beauty authorization may submit PO changes by communicating 
with their Inventory Analyst.

SECTION 11: ULTA.COM DIGITAL PHOTOGRAPHY & CONTENT
GUIDELINES
To request a copy of all guidelines, please reach out to your digital product specialist, or email 
digitalproductmerch@ulta.com 
 
11.1 Images
Standards for Images – Must meet the following site & app PDP standards: 
o Main must be on a transparent background with no shadow or reflection. 
o Main (image shown on product listing pages and first image on PDP) must 
not contain additional text, graphics, or inset images. 
o Most Main are displayed out of package with exceptions for some nail, lashes, 
and accessories. Package may be shown next to the product so long as there 
is no overlapping. Front of package can be used an alternate. 
o Main should contain only what will be shipped to the guest. 
o All must be professional photographs. Must be in focus, professionally lit and 
photographed with consistent angles, realistic color, and smooth edges. 
o Drawings, illustrations or user generated content of the product are not 
allowed. 
o Refrain from dynamic angles. Tools like hair dryers, curling irons, etc. are an 
exception and can be angled. 
o Full product for Main must be in frame. 
o All must not contain gratuitous or confusing additional objects. 
11.2 Videos
• The video should be of professional quality, not blurry and with professional lighting.
• Accepted video content:
o Tutorials and how-to’s of the specific product
o Informative of what the product is and does
o Ideas on use of the specific product
o Educational about how the product is made, health facts, etc., if product has 
certain health/ingredient claims
o Collection videos so long as all items featured in the video are sold on 
Ulta.com.
o See brand primer for additional video file & asset requirements: allowed 
number, sizing, file types, naming, and upload requirements.
 
11.3 Copy Requirements for PIM
• Web Product Name: Product name displays online exactly as entered.
• Value (USD): This is the value of the kit or set, not the retail price. This should only 
be populated when the value of the kit exceeds the retail price.
• Variant Type & Variant Sku Attribute Value: Used to differentiate like items by 
shade, size, scent or other.

14.1 Minimum Packaging Requirements
Ulta Beauty requires all Brand Partners to follow proper shipping requirements to ensure 
product remains in saleable condition and limits safety hazards and damages.
Case Pack 
• All Brand Partners must ensure vendor case packs are properly packaged to 
withstand normal shipping conditions for safe transport, including any regional 
climatic specifications. 
• All items must be packed in sealed fully enclosed corrugated cartons with a minimum 
rating of 200-pound test or ECT 32, to ensure cartons can be stacked and palletized 
without jeopardizing product integrity.
• Glass items, including candles, must be properly secured to 
avoid damage. Each glass item should be properly packaged 
inside an individual carton at the each level (see Unit Pack).
• Delicate items including but not limited to bottles, liquids, 
hairsprays, and mousse should be equipped with proper 
packaging material such as dividers, shipping paper, and/or 
air pillows.
• Case pack must include an additional layer of protection to 
account for prepping the carton by Ulta associates, via a 
box cutter. This protection can include an extra piece of 
cardboard or dunnage. 
Unit Pack 
• All units should be packaged in a way that allows for product and product contents 
to withstand normal retail handling.
• Each sellable unit must have one scannable UPC barcode present. Do not 
include multiple barcodes (e.g. United States and Canada), even if the 
product is sold in multiple countries.
• If the sellable unit contains multiple products under a single UPC barcode, 
labeling should include verbiage, “Single sellable unit. Do not break apart,” 
to avoid product separation during receipt and mitigate shrink.
• Glass items, including candles, must be properly secured to avoid damage. 
Each glass item should be properly packaged inside an individual carton at 
the each level. A scannable UPC must also be present on the outside of 
the individual carton.
• Secondary packaging must be durable enough to withstand normal handling in the 
warehouse. This packaging should not easily tear or break apart and should ensure the 
integrity of the contents during transportation and storage. Approaches to accomplish 
this durability include adding security stickers to corners, ensuring a snug fit within the 
packaging to deter the product from shifting and opening the case, and employing Ulta Beauty Supply Chain Guide – Published March 2025

SECTION 21: INVOICE DISCREPANCY
21.1 Invoice Discrepancy
An invoice discrepancy occurs when there is an observed difference between the Invoice 
submitted by the Brand Partner and the Remittance Advice issued by the Ulta Beauty 
Accounts Payable team. Brand Partners who have not received a Remittance Advice should 
reach out to their designated Accounts Payable Clerk for more information.
Invoice Discrepancy Types & Definitions: 
• Price variance: Pricing on Brand Partner invoice does not match Ulta Beauty AP 
Remittance
• Quantity Variance – Shortage: Quantity of item(s) received at Ulta Beauty DC is 
less than the quantity stated on the Brand Partner invoice.
Invoice Discrepancy Examples: 
• Price Variance 
o Brand Partner fails to update a cost change and has incorrect cost in PIM
o Inaccurate item set up information (incorrect case count/unit of measure)
o Brand Partner invoices Ulta Beauty with incorrect unit of measure (unit of 
measure on the invoice must match the PO)
o PO is set up as a discounted PO but Brand Partner bills Ulta Beauty at full cost
• Quantity Variance – Shortage 
o Brand Partner did not ship product or quantity stated on invoice
o Ulta Beauty DC receiving error
o Carrier freight loss (missing cartons, shrink wrap not intact)
o Mis-ships (wrong item sent or over/under)
o Item set up incorrectly (case pack/inner pack quantity error)
o Damaged upon receipt
o Carton mis-labeled
o Shipment delivered to incorrect address
21.2 Invoice Discrepancy Process 
All Brand Partners have an opportunity to request a Purchase Order delivery audit for 
shortage discrepancies within 30 business days (M-F; No holiday exceptions) of receiving 
the Remittance Advice Form. This process must be initiated before the Chargeback dispute 
process.
Best Practice: Brand Partners should leverage a copy of their Invoice as backup when 
disputing through the Chargeback Process, if the Remittance Advice form has not yet been 
received from A/P.

Performance Tiering:
Tiering creates the opportunity to accurately align chargeback fees with Brands who are 
non-compliant with the regulations outlined in the Ulta Beauty Supply Chain Guide
Performance Tiers are determined by the rolled-up average, by Period, of four key core 
metrics:
o ASN Received 
o Revised Fill Rate
o On Time Performance
o GS1-128 Shipping and Label
• Performance Tiers measures the average performance of a rolling 3-month period
• Performance Tiers are updated by period
• Brands in Performance Tier Zero will not receive chargebacks 
• Tiering will be measured on a rolling 3-month cadence, with a period break in between. 
Example:
o Jan Tier Measures  Sept, Oct, Nov Performance
o Feb Tier Measures  Oct, Nov & Dec Performance
Definitions:
Shipping Accuracy: 
Definition: Measures the ability of Ulta Beauty Distribution Centers to successfully 
receive against a Brand submitted EDI-856 ASN (Advanced Shipping Notice). 
Requirement: ASN’s should be submitted and successfully received prior to the arrival 
of shipments into our DCs. 
o Only one PO per ASN is accepted, and errors must be corrected promptly, 
and prior to the arrival of shipments into our DCs to avoid penalties.
o Accurate GS1-128 shipping labels with scannable barcodes are mandatory for 
all shipping cartons and pallets.
Violations: Chargebacks occur when an Ulta Beauty Distribution Centers cannot 
systematically receive against the Brand Partner submitted ASN, and manual receiving 
is required.
PO Acknowledgement: 
Definition: Measures the successful receipt of the EDI 855 Acknowledgement 
transmitted within two business days of the EDI 850 submission.
Requirement: PO Acknowledgements are required for all transmitted lines on each PO 
(The PO Acknowledgement is a required document for all live PO 850s).
Violations: Chargebacks are assessed when the EDI 855 document is not received 
successfully within those first two business days of the PO – 850 Transmission Date.

25.11 Definitions
3PL (Third Party Logistics) -- Outsourced supply chain management of warehousing 
and fulfillment services.
Article -- Ulta Beauty SAP item number for the purpose of tracking inventory and 
placing orders.
ASN (Advanced Shipment Notice) -- A freight shipment notice furnished to the 
consignee detailing shipment information of a pending delivery including number of 
eaches for each item. 
ASN % - Percentage of ASNs sent by Brand Partner and received by Ulta
Bill of Lading (BOL) -- can be abbreviated as B/L or BOL, is a legal document between 
the shipper of goods and the carrier detailing the type, quantity and destination of the 
goods being carried. The bill of lading also serves as a receipt of shipment when the 
goods are delivered at the predetermined destination.
Cancel Date – The date up to which an issuer reserves the right to cancel the PO and 
refuse delivery. Not a standard date used by Ulta Beauty, but at the discretion of the 
Ulta Beauty Buying and Inventory teams, may also be exchanged.
Carrier -- An entity or individual engaged in the business of transporting goods or 
persons, for a fee.
Chargeback -- A charge levied by the receiving company for violation of the policies 
and procedures.
Claim -- A written demand for payment because of loss or damage alleged to have 
occurred while shipment was in possession of the Carrier.
Collect Freight terms -- Freight charges will be paid by the consignee (Ulta Beauty).
Commercial Terms document - A binding contract denoting a Brand Partner’s 
acceptance of all terms and conditions of conducting business with Ulta Beauty
Consignee -- The person or organization to which the freight is shipped.
Consignor -- The person or organization to which the freight is leaving the facility.
Country of Origin -- The country where an article is produced or in some instances in 
which the majority of the components were produced.
Delivery -- The act of transferring possession, such as the transfer of property from 
consignor to Carrier, one Carrier to another or Carrier to consignee.
Department -- The category number given within ULTA hierarchy that indicates under 
which business the item is sold.Ulta Beauty Supply Chain Guide – Published March 2025
71
Destination -- The place to which a shipment is consigned.
Display -- A fixture that saleable product is placed on
Distribution Center (DC) -- Warehouse or specialized building for the receipt, storage, 
and distribution of goods
Eaches -- A single unit of a product that ULTA orders from the Vendor
EDI (Electronic Data Interchange) -- Computer-to-computer communications of data 
that permits the receiver to perform functions of a business transaction.
European Article Number (EAN) – Unique 13 digit code used to identify an article –
must be in accordance with the approved guidelines of the GS1 standards.
Ensemble -- Multiple saleable items within a single display unit. 
Fast Fulfillment Center (FFC) - A limited assortment facility that supports eCommerce 
fulfillment for a specific market
Fill Rate % -- Ratio of quantity received versus the quantity requested on the PO.
FCL (Full Container Load) -- Whole container that is usually intended for one consignee.
Freight -- Merchandise or goods transported by truck, train, ship, or aircraft.
Freight Bill -- Document for common Carrier shipment. It gives the description of the

destination or collect freight bills.
FTL -- Full truck load or full trailer load.
GS1 (Global Standards 1) – A not-for-profit organization that develops and maintains
